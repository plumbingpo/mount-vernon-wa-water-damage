Pipes that burst rank among the most frequent causes of water damage, and they can do devastating harm to a building. At 4 to 8 gallons a minute, just one burst can send 240 to 480 gallons each hour into your ceilings, walls and flooring. Whether the cause is freezing temperatures, aging plumbing, excessive water pressure, corrosion or physical damage, a burst pipe demands immediate emergency response to limit the damage water does to structural materials, flooring, personal belongings and building systems.

Day or night, Mount Vernon Restoration responds to burst pipe emergencies across Mount Vernon and the nearby communities. Technicians on our team assist with finding and closing the water supply, while licensed plumbers we coordinate with handle a proper plumbing repair. As soon as the flow is shut down, extraction starts: portable and truck-mounted units pull standing water out of every affected area as fast as we can.

Following extraction, our structural drying process addresses all moisture-affected materials, including drywall, insulation, wood framing, subflooring and finished flooring. Gravity carries the water through walls, floors and ceilings, so damage from one burst commonly shows up in several rooms and on different floors. Hidden moisture leads to mold and a deteriorating structure, so we use moisture detection equipment and thermal imaging to map the water's path and make sure all affected areas get dried.

When to Call for Burst Pipe Repair in Mount Vernon

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Hissing or rushing noises in the walls with every tap turned off
  • Tap water that turns brown, yellow or cloudy
  • Stale or earthy smells coming from a cabinet or wall cavity
  • Wet spots, bulging or staining on walls or ceilings
  • Puddles on the floor that you cannot trace to anything obvious
  • Exposed pipes that drip, mist or shoot water
  • A sudden drop in water pressure throughout your property

How to Prevent Burst Pipe Repair Problems

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Pressure higher than 80 PSI wears on pipes and their fittings, so test yours from time to time
  • Every few years, ask a licensed plumber to look over the system for corrosion and worn parts
  • Find the main shutoff valve now and practice closing it so you can do it fast
  • If your home still has polybutylene or aging galvanized steel lines, replace them before one gives out
  • Install water leak detection sensors near water heaters and main supply lines
  • When it freezes, let taps drip and open the cabinets under sinks to let warm air reach the pipes
